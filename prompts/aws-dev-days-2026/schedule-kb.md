# AWS Startup Dev Day (Melbourne) 2026

## Event Overview

AWS Startup Dev Day Melbourne 2026 is a free, one-day, in-person event held on Monday the 27th of July 2026, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, at Level 13, 555 Collins Street, Melbourne, Victoria 3000, Australia.

It is an event for builders, technical startup founders, and CTOs who want to go deep on building with AI, designing architecture, and the technology decisions that define a growing startup. AWS Startup Dev Day returns to Melbourne joined by OpenAI, who deliver the opening keynote on building and shipping at the frontier of AI. Sessions run at the 300 (Advanced) and 400 (Expert) level.

Spaces are limited and attendees are selected to bring together a strong cohort. The event is run in partnership with OpenAI, alongside technology partners including Deepgram, Neo4j, Kinde, Plerion, Cloud Dialogues, and i14.

## Two Content Tracks

Attendees choose between two tracks throughout the day:

1. Technical Founders — the decisions that scale a company: AI strategy, data pipelines, security as a business risk, and architecting for growth.
2. Builders — hands-on coding agents, agentic analytics, multi-tenant SaaS, and build-alongside workshops.

## Voice AI Demo at Dev Day

We are showcasing a live real-time voice AI agent demo. The demo shows a cascaded voice agent architecture running entirely on AWS. Your speech is transcribed by Deepgram in real time, an LLM on Amazon Bedrock reasons about your question and generates a response, and text-to-speech converts that response into natural sounding speech. Pipecat by Daily orchestrates the full pipeline end to end, and Tavus renders the visual avatar you see on screen.

You are talking to this demo right now. There is also a dedicated Voice AI Demo session in the Builders track at 2:45 PM, and a partner session, Building Production-Ready Voice Agents with AWS and Deepgram, in the Technical Founders track at 1:00 PM.
