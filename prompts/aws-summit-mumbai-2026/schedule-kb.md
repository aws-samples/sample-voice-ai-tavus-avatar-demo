# AWS Summit Mumbai 2026

## Event Overview

AWS Summit Mumbai 2026 is a free one-day event held on Thursday the 28th of May 2026 at the Jio World Convention Centre, also known as JWCC, in Bandra Kurla Complex, also known as BKC, Mumbai, India.

The summit is free to attend and covers the latest in cloud and AI innovation, from agentic AI to serverless computing. Attendees can engage with industry leaders, AWS experts, interactive workshops, and customer showcases.

## Expo Hall

The main expo is located in Jasmine Hall at the Jio World Convention Centre. It features hands-on demos, expert conversations, and innovations across cloud, AI, and industry solutions.

## Zones

### AWS Village
Cutting-edge demos built using AWS services. Hands-on experiences with AI services including Amazon Nova, Data and Analytics, Migration and Modernisation, and the newly launched Kiro. Attendees can connect directly with AWS experts.

### AWS for Industries Zone
Industry-tailored cloud solutions across retail, financial services, media and entertainment, and more. Features dedicated industry pods, interactive demos, subject matter experts, and real-world implementation success stories.

### Startup Zone
Hub for startup founders, investors, and builders. Technical demos, AWS Startup Experts, and showcases from promising regional startups. Covers AWS Activate and resources for scaling from inception to IPO.

### Developer Community Zone
Peer-led sessions, technical deep-dives, career connects, Dev Chats, and AWS Cloud Club and User Group showcases. Covers developer tooling, serverless architectures, and modern application development.

### Training and Certification Zone
Card Clash activation, AWS Skill Builder information, and expert-led Lightning Talks for all skill levels. Covers certification pathways, self-paced learning resources, and AWS Training experts.

### Innovation Arcade
Interactive zone celebrating AWS's 20 years of innovation. Hands-on experiences, immersive demos, and playful discovery of current and future technologies.

## Keynote

The keynote is the main opening session. Space is limited and keynote access is not guaranteed by registration. The keynote is livestreamed in the Expo and in breakout rooms for overflow. It features regional leaders sharing how AWS empowers builders and businesses to innovate from India for the world.

## Sessions

All session seating is first-come, first-served. There is no reserved seating so arrive early for popular sessions.

## Practical Information

Entry requires a unique QR code sent 24 to 48 hours before the event via email and again on the morning of the event via SMS. A valid government-approved photo ID is required for badge pickup. Minimum age is 18 years.

The event is free and includes access to all sessions and the Expo.

Dress code is business casual. Suits, hoodies, and jeans are all welcome. No shorts or slippers.

For questions, contact aws-india-marketing@amazon.com.

## Voice AI Demo at the Summit

We are showcasing a live real-time voice AI agent demo at the AWS booth. The demo title is Building Real-time Voice Agents Using Deepgram, Pipecat and AWS.

This demo shows a cascaded voice agent architecture running entirely on AWS. Your speech is transcribed by Deepgram Nova 3 in real time. An LLM on Amazon Bedrock reasons about your question and generates a response. Deepgram Aura converts that response into natural sounding speech. Pipecat by Daily orchestrates the full pipeline end to end. Tavus renders the visual avatar you see on screen.

You are talking to this demo right now.
