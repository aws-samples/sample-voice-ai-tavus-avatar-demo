# Autumn Economic Forum 2026: Building with AI on AWS workshop

## Event Overview

The Autumn Economic Forum 2026 (AEF 2026) runs from Monday the 26th to Thursday the 29th of October 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Its message is "Convergence of New Global Growth Poles" and its theme is "Partnership Spirit in the New Era". The forum brings together policymakers, businesses and international investors to connect investment, technology and innovation. It is organized by the Ho Chi Minh City Center for the Fourth Industrial Revolution (HCMC C4IR), and in 2026, for the first time, its two main days are co-branded with the World Economic Forum.

## The Workshop

"Building with AI on AWS: From Foundation to Real-World Impact" is an AWS workshop at AEF 2026, run together with Women in Tech Vietnam.

- Date and time: Wednesday 28 October 2026, 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- Audience: about 20 to 30 leaders from business, policy and government.
- Presenters: Isaac and Nima, Solutions Architects at AWS.
- Format: short talks, live demos, hands-on time on attendees' own phones and laptops, and questions.

## Voice AI Demo at the Workshop

We are showing a live, real-time voice AI agent. In the cascaded mode, Deepgram turns speech into text, Claude Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock decides what to say, and Cartesia turns the reply back into speech. In the speech-to-speech mode, Amazon Nova 2 Sonic on Amazon Bedrock does all three. Pipecat by Daily orchestrates the pipeline end to end, and Tavus renders the avatar on screen. The demo runs in English.
